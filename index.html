Processing times

Orders are typically processed and prepared for dispatch within 1–3 business days, excluding weekends and public holidays. During launches, promotions and holiday periods, processing may take a little longer; if your order will be delayed, we'll let you know by email.

Shipping rates

Orders with a merchandise subtotal of $75 or more qualify for free shipping. Orders below $75 carry a flat shipping charge of $7.50. The shipping charge that applies to your order is always shown in your bag and at checkout before you place your order.

Delivery times & tracking

Delivery times depend on your destination and the carrier's network. Once your order ships, we'll send a shipping confirmation to the email address on your account with tracking details where available. Delivery estimates are provided by carriers and are not guaranteed.

International orders, customs & duties

We ship worldwide. Orders shipped outside the United States may be subject to customs duties, import taxes and brokerage fees charged by the destination country. These charges are not included in your order total, are collected by the carrier or customs authority, and are the responsibility of the recipient. Customs processing can add time to delivery.

Shipping address

Please check that your shipping address is complete and accurate before placing your order. If you notice a mistake, email us as soon as possible — we'll do our best to update it before your order ships, but we can't change an address once a parcel is with the carrier.

Lost, delayed or damaged parcels

If your tracking hasn't updated for an extended period, your parcel arrives damaged, or something is missing, please contact us with your order number and, where relevant, photos of the packaging and items. We'll work with the carrier to resolve it.

Pre-order & coming-soon items

Items marked "Coming Soon" are not yet available to purchase. When they launch, any estimated dispatch date will be shown on the product page.

Returns & Refunds

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Our promise

We want you to love every NURVA piece. If something isn't right, you may return eligible items within 14 days of delivery for a refund or exchange.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with all original tags attached and in their original packaging.
  • Items must be free of makeup, perfume, deodorant, pet hair and other marks or odours.
  • Return requests must be made within 14 days of the delivery date shown in your tracking information.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene reasons, undergarments and inner caps cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty or incorrect. Items marked as final sale at the time of purchase cannot be returned or exchanged.

How to start a return

  • Email Enquire@nurvahijabs.com with your order number, the item(s) you'd like to return and the reason for your return.
  • We'll review your request and reply with return instructions, including where to send your item(s).
  • Please don't send items back without first receiving return instructions from us, as we may be unable to identify or process them.

Return shipping

Unless your item arrived faulty, damaged or incorrect, return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility and original shipping charges are non-refundable. We recommend using a trackable shipping service, as we can't be responsible for returns lost in transit. International customers are responsible for any customs fees on return parcels.

Refunds

Once your return arrives, we'll inspect it and email you to confirm whether it's approved. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days of approval. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to post the refund to your account. Customs duties and import taxes paid on international orders are generally not refundable by NURVA.

Exchanges

Would you prefer a different colour or style? Mention it in your return request and we'll arrange an exchange where stock allows. If the item you'd like isn't available, we'll issue a refund instead.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives faulty, damaged or incorrect, please email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund, including shipping costs, at no extra cost to you.

Your statutory rights

This policy does not affect any rights you may have under the consumer protection laws that apply where you live.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NURVA HIJABS ("NURVA", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares personal information when you visit nurvahijabs.com, create an account, save items, place an order or contact us. If you have any questions, email Enquire@nurvahijabs.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your name, email address and password when you create a NURVA account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us.
  • Order information: the products you order, quantities, prices, order totals, order number and order status.
  • Contact and shipping information: your name, email address, phone number and shipping address, including any shipping details you choose to save to your account.
  • Communications: the content of emails and messages you send us, such as return requests or questions about an order.

Information stored on your device

  • Shopping bag: the items in your bag are stored in your browser's local storage so your bag is kept between visits.
  • Saved items and account conveniences: your saved items and a copy of your recent orders and shipping details may be stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use.
  • Sign-in session: when you sign in, our authentication provider stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the services that host and deliver our website (including our hosting provider, our fonts provider and our content delivery network) receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and the pages requested, as part of delivering the site to you. We do not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, keep you signed in, and help you reset your password.
  • To process, fulfil and ship your orders, and to show your order history.
  • To send transactional emails, such as account verification, password resets, order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • To respond to your questions and handle returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To save your preferences, such as saved items and shipping details, so checkout is faster.
  • To keep our website secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and comply with legal obligations such as tax and accounting requirements.

We will only send you marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share personal information only with service providers that help us run our store, and only as needed for them to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase — account authentication, secure database storage for accounts and orders, and server functions that send order emails.
  • Our email delivery provider — to send account and order emails.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — delivery of fonts and website code to your browser.
  • Shipping carriers — your name, address and, where required, phone number, to deliver your order.
  • Payment processors — when online payment is available, your payment details will be collected and processed directly by our payment processor. NURVA does not store full card numbers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, customers or others, or in connection with a sale or reorganisation of our business.

Cookies and local storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage, rather than advertising cookies, to remember your shopping bag, saved items and sign-in session. These are necessary for the store to work as you'd expect. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings; doing so will empty your bag and sign you out.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil your order, handle returns and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

How we protect your information

Account and order data is stored with Supabase using encrypted connections and access controls that restrict each customer to their own account and order records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

International customers

NURVA is based in the United States and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. If you shop with us from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, and processed in, countries whose data protection laws may differ from those where you live. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your information to perform our contract with you (for example, to fulfil your order), to comply with legal obligations, on the basis of our legitimate interests in operating and securing our store, or with your consent where required.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update your name and saved shipping information in your account at any time. To make any other request, email Enquire@nurvahijabs.com from the email address on your account. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

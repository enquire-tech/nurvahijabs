Returns & Refunds

Last updated: September 2026

Our promise

If you're not fully happy with your NURVA order, we want to make it right. Items may be returned within 14 days of delivery, provided they are unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging with tags attached.

How to start a return

  • Email Enquire@nurvahijabs.com with your order number and reason for return.
  • We'll confirm your return and provide instructions on where to send your item.
  • Once received and inspected, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Exchanges

Need a different colour or size? Let us know in your return request and we'll arrange an exchange where stock allows.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene reasons, undergarments and inner caps cannot be returned unless faulty.
